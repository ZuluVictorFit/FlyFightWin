Operation Fly, Fight, Win

Three lift days. Three run days. VDOT-based paces. The first lift family runs through week 12, then week 13 starts a second triphasic cycle with new primary lifts. Test work is the 2-mile run, 1-minute push-ups and 1-minute sit-ups.
