# Prescription and equipment review

The application preserves the approved PDF. These items need athlete/coach review before changing any prescription:

- **Heavy front carry:** confirm Zack has an implement appropriate to the prescribed heavy sandbag front carry. The newer private equipment inventory conflicts with the prescription. The app flags this; it does not substitute a ball or relabel a light bag as heavy. Hannah's preferred carry implements remain in her private imported baseline.
- **VOLTRA:** verify availability, firmware, anchor setup and manufacturer instructions for the named modes. No device control or unverified setting is implemented. Device settings and simulated yards remain separate from physical sled loads and distances.
- **Reduced weeks:** weeks 4 and 8 retain one top set plus two back-offs where prescribed. This is intentionally preserved despite the general preference for more accessory sets. All optional lower finishers retain their prescribed three sets.
- **Pace reference:** Zack defaults to the approved PDF's 47.2 reference; historical tests remain selectable, without silently choosing the latest. Hannah defaults to a new-block two-mile test once logged. A pinned historical reference takes precedence until changed. Nonmaximal runs do not create VDOT references.
- **Strength estimates:** initial percentage bands are advisory, labeled provisional, and reduced for lower RPE caps. They are not new prescriptions. Unestablished lifts require an RPE-guided ramp. Imported provisional ranges retain their provenance and are only used for matching rep exposures; old or different-lift tests never become tested maxes.
- **Variants:** performed Pendlay rows remain distinct from programmed bent-over rows. Floor cleans do not establish hang-clean maxes. Logging a different variant records what happened; it does not authorize a program substitution.

No PDF prescription divergences were found in the independent cell-by-cell audit. No program decisions requiring a silent substitution were made. No production deployment or merge is part of this change.
